DCF Valuation

Discounted Cash Flow. Σ CFₜ/(1+r)ᵗ + Terminal Value.

How It Works

DCF projects future cash flows, discounts them to present value, adds a terminal value for the period beyond the projection horizon. Enterprise value = PV(cash flows) + PV(terminal value). Equity value = EV × 0.85 (typical discount for lack of marketability).
